The free session

Give your agent a project.
Not just another prompt.

We’ll work through practical examples together and explore how the same patterns apply to your own public, non-confidential project.

No programming experience required. This is hands-on project work, not a beginner chatbot tutorial or an installation clinic.

  1. Define the outcome.
    Turn a broad idea into concrete deliverables.
  2. Set up durable context.
    Provide instructions, files, and authority boundaries that carry across stages.
  3. Guide longer-running work.
    Use checkpoints and human decisions without constantly starting over.
  4. Check what happened.
    Evaluate quality, completion, traceability, and correction burden.

Bring your agent.
Bring your curiosity.

Lawyers and other professionals are welcome. Bring a laptop and a working agent environment you already use: Codex, Claude Code, Claude desktop/Cowork, Cursor, or a comparable tool.

Bring a non-confidential project idea, or use one of our examples. Please do not bring confidential client or organizational information into shared exercises.

The session is free. Any charges for your own agent tools remain yours.
